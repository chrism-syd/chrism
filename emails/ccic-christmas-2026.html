THE 2026 COLLECTION

Our collection features 16 unique card designs, carefully created to balance timeless sacred art with modern aesthetics that appeal to Catholic families of all ages.

Each box contains:

• 12 Christmas cards

• 12 envelopes

• A faith-centred design and Scripture message

PROUDLY DESIGNED
& PRINTED IN CANADA

For many years, the program relied on suppliers outside Canada. Fluctuating exchange rates, tariffs, and international shipping created rising costs and delays.

The 2026 collection is now 100% designed and printed in Canada, helping us maintain reliable quality, stable pricing, and a stronger domestic supply chain.
BUILDING FOR THE FUTURE

We are exploring the possibility of offering Christmas cards in French and other languages in future collections.

To help us understand the need, please let us know which language your council or parish would be interested in and approximately how many boxes you might order.

Your feedback will help guide future card offerings. Simply reply to this email to let us know.
